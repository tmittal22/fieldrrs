## The correction chain

For each water scan, with its angle-matched sky scan and the panel reference stored with it:

1. E_d = π L_panel / R_panel, R_panel = 0.99.
2. ρ(θ_v) = 0.028 · R_F(θ_v) / R_F(40°) (Mobley 1999, scaled by the Fresnel ratio), θ_v = |tilt Y|.
3. R_rs = (L_t − ρ L_sky) / E_d.
4. Glint: a spectrally flat ε is subtracted so that R_rs(780)/R_rs(870) matches the Ruddick et al. (2006) similarity ratio for turbid water.
5. FINAL: iterative amplitude-normalised mean of the kept scans (THEORY_SCALED_MEAN.md).
