# Why LOC2 is three stations, not one

`LOC2_66.89677N_162.57953W/FLENS8_FOV08/` holds the raw 23 scans as recorded (unchanged,
still the source of truth). `analyse_water_scans.py` run against it found the 12 water
scans are **not one population** (spectral-angle matrix, `w1_contact_sheet.png`,
`w3_groups_and_glint.png` in that folder). Rather than average three different things
into one number, the scans are split by symlink (no data duplicated, one source of
truth) into three stations that follow the existing `LOC<n>_<lat>N_<lon>W/` convention:

| station | water scans | what it is | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LOC2a** | 00030–00038 (9, incl. 00035) | the main open-water population | internal shape spread 1.08°, all but one scan clean |
| **LOC2b** | 00027, 00028, 00029 (3) | disturbed/turbid water at station start | internally tight (0.53°), 2.6–4.7° from LOC2a, does NOT collapse under a glint correction |
| **LOC2c** | 00043, 00044 (2, role="land") | a floating algae mat | opaque-target reflectance, not R_rs |

Every station also gets the 9 sky scans and the panel context, symlinked, so each can be
processed as a self-contained folder with the ordinary pipeline.

## LOC2b: why this is "different water", not "bad data"

Full evidence in `LOC2_66.89677N_162.57953W/FLENS8_FOV08/analysis/water_scans/REPORT.txt`
and the water-scan figures there. Summary:

1. **Not view angle.** The apparent correlation with tilt across all 12 scans
   (r=+0.69, p=0.01) vanishes to r=+0.001 (p=0.999) once the 3 scans are set aside —
   an artefact of them being both blue-elevated and higher-tilt, not a geometric effect.
2. **Not the sky pairing.** Forcing every scan onto one fixed sky barely moves them.
3. **Not the panel.** Panel shape is bitwise identical between the two groups.
4. **No single artefact fits.** Sky reflection removes ~51% of the difference; a steep
   mineral-backscatter shape removes ~47%. Both partial — consistent with a real,
   physically mixed signal rather than one clean correction term.
5. **The timing is the decisive evidence.** 00027/28/29 are the first three water scans
   at this station, taken consecutively, range unchanged (0.876 m) into the scan that
   follows. That is the signature of sediment disturbed at the start of the station
   (wading in, a boat) settling out over roughly two minutes — a real, transient water
   state, not a measurement artefact.

**Consequence for LOC2a**: once LOC2b's 3 scans are removed, LOC2a's own
`analyse_water_scans.py` run flags only 00035 (glint, not a population split) — see
`LOC2a_66.89677N_162.57953W/FLENS8_FOV08/analysis/water_scans/SCAN_00035_GLINT.md` for
that one, including what was and was not done about it.

## LOC2c: the algae mat is not R_rs

00043/00044 were classified `"land"` by `process_field_day.classify()` on the basis of a
radiance ratio open water does not show — not because they are dry ground. The
photographs (this folder and LOC2a's contact sheet) show floating clumps, one mid-water
and one against a dock. They are processed as opaque-target **reflectance**
(`R = R_panel · L_target/L_panel`, no ρ, no sky subtraction — see
`analyse_algae_mat.py`), the same physical treatment LOC1 used for its own algae-on-
concrete target, and for the same reason: for an opaque-enough surface the sky is
illumination, not glint. This is a genuinely different physical quantity from R_rs and
must not be compared to it in magnitude without accounting for that — see
`loc1_loc2abc_overplot.png` (below) for how that is kept honest.

## The overplot

`COMPARISON/loc1_loc2abc_overplot.png` — LOC1, LOC2a, LOC2b on one R_rs axis (left
panel; comparable quantities), LOC2c's reflectance on its own separate axis (right
panel; explicitly labelled as a different physical quantity, never merged with R_rs).

**What it shows**: LOC2a and LOC2b overlap almost everywhere in shape *except* the blue
end (400–490 nm), where LOC2b sits visibly above LOC2a — the same elevated-blue
signature found independently in the per-scan spectral analysis, now visible directly in
the final products. LOC1 sits below both LOC2 sub-stations in the blue and has a
different balance between its 570 nm and 700 nm peaks. Headline R_rs(555): LOC1 0.00865,
LOC2a 0.01081, LOC2b 0.01068 sr⁻¹.

## Reproduce

```bash
D=Data_NatureSpec/2026_Aug_16/by_location
for S in LOC2a LOC2b; do
    L=$D/${S}_66.89677N_162.57953W/FLENS8_FOV08
    python verify_field_calcs.py "$L"
    python analyse_location.py "$L"
    python analyse_water_scans.py "$L"
    python make_interactive_report.py "$L"
done
python analyse_algae_mat.py $D/LOC2c_66.89677N_162.57953W/FLENS8_FOV08
```

The symlinks were built once, by hand, from `LOC2_66.89677N_162.57953W/FLENS8_FOV08/`;
they are not regenerated by any script, so if LOC2's raw data ever changes, rebuild them.
