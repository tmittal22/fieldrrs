## Two things are fitted here

**1. The mean shape** (), hyperspectral 400–700 nm, n=301:

| | value | ± (from the 1.7 % shape uncertainty) |
|---|---|---|
| M_φ | 11.47 | ±2.2 % |
| a_dg(443) | 1.254 m⁻¹ | ±1.7 % |
| b_bp(443) | 0.0836 m⁻¹ | ±1.1 % |

**2. Each scan separately**, using its own angle-matched sky (, 12 pairs):

| | mean across scans | sd |
|---|---|---|
| M_φ | 11.45 | 13 % |
| a_dg(443) | 1.254 | 8 % |
| b_bp(443) | 0.0847 | 11 % |

The two agree to **0.2 %** on every parameter, which is a real consistency check: fitting
the mean and averaging the fits are different operations and there was no guarantee they
would land together. The per-scan sd (8–13 %) tracks the 11 % amplitude spread, as it
should — the scans are the same water at different concentrations.

## What to quote

- **a_dg(443) = 1.25 ± 0.10 m⁻¹** and **b_bp(443) = 0.085 ± 0.009 m⁻¹** — the
  scan-to-scan sd, which is real variability rather than fit error.
- **Not chlorophyll.** M_φ = 11.5 is precise (±2 %) but that is precision, not accuracy:
  the model still misfits by 10.7 % RMS hyperspectral, and sweeping S_dg over 0.014–0.022
  moves it 2.4 → 20.9. A Case-1 a*_φ on Case-2 water does not become right by being
  well-conditioned.
