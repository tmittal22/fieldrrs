  READING: the spread is REAL WATER, not measurement error. The composition is
  near-constant (shape stable to 3.2 %) while the AMOUNT of scattering material
  changes by a factor 1.45 between scans, with no time trend, over a footprint of
  ~0.16 m. That is spatial patchiness in suspended load, which is what a turbid
  nearshore surface does.

  confidence than absolute magnitudes at this site.
